EXECUTIVE VERDICT

ProofyX is a credible CIIC incubation candidate and a weak investment candidate — today. That is the right order to pitch in.

  • Fit with CIIC's mandate: strong on paper. CIIC's own mission is to "nurture early-stage startups among students, faculty, and alumni" and its vision is to be a "top university-based technology incubator, fostering entrepreneurial students into profitable startups" CIIC-VERIFIED.   • Fit with CIIC's actual portfolio: weaker. Its student portfolio is dominated by life-sciences and nano-formulation ventures; software/AI startups are a minority and almost all external or faculty-led CIIC-VERIFIED, 2023 portfolio snapshot. ProofyX would be one of very few student-founded AI-security companies at CIIC. That is a differentiator and a reason CIIC's committee may have fewer reference points — you will need to educate them on the market.
  • Money, honestly: CIIC does not appear to invest its own capital in early student startups. Every funding instance I could verify was CIIC acting as implementing agency for a government scheme (SISFS, GENESIS EiR, SAMRIDH, NIDHI-PRAYAS) CIIC-VERIFIED.   • The model is weaker on fresh samples than the headline suggests. A 40-image in-domain spot check this week scored 62.5% at the product's own threshold, and 24 out-of-domain images scored 46–50%. Slide 7 of the CIIC deck states both. Demo only with verified in-domain samples (see §5B-ii).
