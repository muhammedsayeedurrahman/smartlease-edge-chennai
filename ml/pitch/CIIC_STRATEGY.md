# EXECUTIVE VERDICT

**ProofyX is a credible CIIC incubation candidate and a weak investment candidate — today.** That is the right order to pitch in.

- **Fit with CIIC's mandate:** strong on paper. CIIC's own mission is to "nurture early-stage startups among students, faculty, and alumni" and its vision is to be a "top university-based technology incubator, fostering entrepreneurial students into profitable startups" **[CIIC-VERIFIED]**. CIIC's NIDHI-PRAYAS page lists **AI, IoT, ML, Big Data and Cyber Security** as focus domains under Industry 4.0 **[CIIC-VERIFIED]**. It runs an **Intel AI Lab, Cloud Computing Lab** and an **IHFC (IIT Delhi) Co-Innovation Centre** on campus **[CIIC-VERIFIED]**. A Crescent-built AI/cybersecurity product is exactly what those assets exist for.
- **Fit with CIIC's actual portfolio:** weaker. Its student portfolio is dominated by life-sciences and nano-formulation ventures; software/AI startups are a minority and almost all external or faculty-led **[CIIC-VERIFIED, 2023 portfolio snapshot]**. ProofyX would be one of very few student-founded AI-security companies at CIIC. That is a differentiator *and* a reason CIIC's committee may have fewer reference points — you will need to educate them on the market.
- **Money, honestly:** CIIC does not appear to invest its own capital in early student startups. Every funding instance I could verify was CIIC acting as **implementing agency for a government scheme** (SISFS, GENESIS EiR, SAMRIDH, NIDHI-PRAYAS) **[CIIC-VERIFIED]**. As of September 2026: **SISFS is closed** (final startup deadline 31 May 2026, no successor announced) **[GOV-VERIFIED]**; **GENESIS EiR 3.0 closed 3 Sept 2026** **[GOV-VERIFIED]**; **TANSEED 8.0 closed 30 Jan 2026**, 9.0 not announced; **NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0** is the live scheme, with new guidelines in 2026 — but whether CIIC has been re-designated as a PRAYAS 2.0 centre is **UNKNOWN**. So the realistic ask is: *incubate us now, and put us in the queue for the next PRAYAS / GENESIS / TANSEED window* — with ₹8 lakh as the milestone-based requirement.
- **The model is weaker on fresh samples than the headline suggests.** A 40-image in-domain spot check this week scored 62.5% at the product's own threshold, and 24 out-of-domain images scored 46–50%. Slide 7 of the CIIC deck states both. Demo only with verified in-domain samples (see §5B-ii).
- **The single biggest risk is not the pitch — it is your public website.** `ai-authenticity-checker.vercel.app` currently claims "99.4% Accurate", "< 2 Seconds", "12 AI models… in real time", "court-admissible forensic dossiers with mathematical certainty and cryptographic custody chains" **[REPO]**. Your own deck says 82.5% on 332 samples, 564 ms per image, zero customers. A committee member who opens the site during your pitch will conclude the team overclaims. **Fix the landing page before you send anyone the link.**

# 3. Current CIIC Programs — would ProofyX qualify NOW?

## 3.1 NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0 — the best-fit grant, with one trap

**[GOV-VERIFIED — DST NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0 Programme Guidelines 2026]**
- Purpose: proof-of-concept grant to convert an idea/innovation into a prototype / advanced prototype / MVP. "Proposals for products having crossed MVP level will not be eligible."
- Amount: up to ₹20 lakh (PRAYAS Centre) or ₹40 lakh (Advance PRAYAS Centre, for deep-tech). Project term 15 months.
- Applicant: an individual innovator without a registered startup **or** an early-stage startup incorporated ≤5 years, ≥51% Indian-held, turnover <₹1 crore.
- Full-time engagement required; students need an institutional NOC that also confirms IP vests with the innovator/startup.
- IP stays with the innovator/startup; **the centre may not take equity or IP as a precondition**.
- Optional sustenance allowance up to 20% of the grant for founders with no other income.
- Preference: bootstrapped/co-investment, clear commercialisation roadmap, young innovators, **deep-tech innovators/researchers, innovators with patents**.
- **Exclusion:** *"Projects related to Pure Software development, e-Commerce, Service solutions, App based solutions"* are not considered.

**Would ProofyX qualify NOW? → POSSIBLY.**
- *For:* you are exactly at the intended stage (working prototype, pre-MVP calibration); AI models are R&D, not "pure software"; CIIC's own PRAYAS page lists AI/ML and Cyber Security as domains.
- *Against:* a reviewer could read a web app as "app-based solution". You must frame the proposal as **prototype development of a novel detection model (CorefakeNet shared-backbone multi-head architecture) with measured evaluation**, not as a website. A provisional patent filing strengthens this materially.
- *Unknown:* whether CIIC has been designated a PRAYAS 2.0 PC/APC after the May–June 2026 incubator call. **Ask.**

# 20. Final Verdict

1. **Strong CIIC fit?** Yes on mission and domain; moderate on portfolio precedent; contingent on incorporation and honest claims.
2. **Top 3 advantages:** working measured product; Crescent-built in CIIC's listed AI/cyber domain; explicit Indian regulatory tailwind (IT Rules 2026, RBI Aug 2025).

5. **Evidence that most increases chances:** a committed evaluation artefact (confusion matrix, ROC, robustness); DPIIT number; three interview summaries; one draft pilot MoU; a corrected website.
6. **Is ₹8 lakh right?** Yes — credible, below every scheme ceiling, milestone-mapped. Be ready to accept ₹5 lakh with a re-scope and to state ₹10 lakh as the "up to".
7. **First funding path:** whichever CIIC confirms is open — most likely **NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0** (if CIIC is a 2.0 centre) or the **next GENESIS EiR cohort**; MSME Innovative as the alternate; TANSEED at Stage 2.
8. **First customer:** NBFC/fintech video-KYC fraud teams (paid), with one cyber-crime validation pilot (unpaid) via CIIC.
9. **Deck changes:** ₹ not $; ₹8 lakh not $2.5M; India-first problem and why-now; Why-CIIC slide; first-customer slide; Indian competitors; real screenshots; ladder instead of five-year ARR.
10. **Product changes before pitching:** website claims; adversarial eval run; calibration fix at least diagnosed with a plan; committed results file; real demo path rehearsed offline.
